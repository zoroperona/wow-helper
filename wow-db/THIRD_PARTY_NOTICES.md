# Third-party components

The exporter references these upstream packages without modifying or vendoring their source:

- TACTSharp 0.2.0-alpha, MIT: https://github.com/wowdev/TACTSharp
- DBCD 2.3.0, MIT: https://github.com/wowdev/DBCD
- Microsoft.Data.Sqlite 10.0.11, MIT: https://github.com/dotnet/efcore

Runtime metadata is downloaded separately:

- WoWDBDefs code is BSD-3-Clause and its definitions are CC BY-SA 4.0: https://github.com/wowdev/WoWDBDefs
- TACTKeys is fetched into the local cache and is not bundled: https://github.com/wowdev/TACTKeys

World of Warcraft client data is owned by its respective rights holders. Generated snapshots are not included in this repository.
